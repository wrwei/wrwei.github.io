A term with two meanings lists both. The numbers in the last column are sections of the modules: 8.3 is Module 8, Section 8.3. The first is where the term is introduced; the others are where it is defined precisely, used in a new setting, or extended to several variables. The symbols come first, in [Notation](#notation); the formulas are on the [formula sheet](formulas_EN.html).
