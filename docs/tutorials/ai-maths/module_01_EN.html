Probabilities on a finite vocabulary

A language model returns a distribution over its vocabulary at each prefix. All probabilities are nonnegative and the sum over the vocabulary is one. Treat a token as a discrete outcome. In practice a token can be a word fragment, punctuation, or a special symbol. Here we use letters and a stop token.

For events AAA and BBB with P(B)>0,P(B)>0\mathclose{\text{,}}P(B)>0, P(A∣B)=P(A∩B)/P(B).P(A\mid B)=P(A\cap B)/P(B)\mathclose{\text{.}}P(A∣B)=P(A∩B)/P(B). The denominator restricts the sample space to outcomes with B.B\mathclose{\text{.}}B. The marginal P(A)P(A)P(A) can be recovered by summing joint probabilities over a partition. For a binary partition, P(A)=P(A∩B)+P(A∩Bc).P(A)=P(A\cap B)+P(A\cap B^\mathsf c)\mathclose{\text{.}}P(A)=P(A∩B)+P(A∩Bc).

Score a continuation

Definition 1.1The probability chain rule
P(t1,…,tT)=∏i=1TP(ti∣t1,…,ti−1).P(t_1,\ldots,t_T)=\prod_{i=1}^T P(t_i\mid t_1,\ldots,t_{i-1}). P(t1​,…,tT​)=i=1∏T​P(ti​∣t1​,…,ti−1​).

For a given prompt, condition every factor on that prompt as well. Repeatedly expand the probability of a prefix and its next token as a product. Factors for the specified path suffice, even though each factor comes from a distribution over all possible next tokens. For an exact completed sequence, include the probability of the stop token. Stop is unnecessary when the question is whether the sequence begins with a prefix.

The probability of beginning with ABABAB is 3/20.3/20\mathclose{\text{.}}3/20. The probability of emitting exactly ABABAB and stopping is 1/10.1/10\mathclose{\text{.}}1/10.

Reverse the condition

Suppose a token was produced by one of two sources, SSS or T.T\mathclose{\text{.}}T. If P(S)=1/4,P(S)=1/4\mathclose{\text{,}}P(S)=1/4, P(A∣S)=3/5P(A\mid S)=3/5P(A∣S)=3/5 and P(A∣T)=1/5,P(A\mid T)=1/5\mathclose{\text{,}}P(A∣T)=1/5, then P(A)=3/10.P(A)=3/10\mathclose{\text{.}}P(A)=3/10. Bayes’ rule divides the joint probability by that marginal, giving P(S∣A)=(3/20)/(3/10)=1/2.P(S\mid A)=(3/20)/(3/10)=1/2\mathclose{\text{.}}P(S∣A)=(3/20)/(3/10)=1/2.
